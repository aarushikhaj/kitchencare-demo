KitchenCare

A clickable prototype for chefs: sign up and get a quote, then report a broken machine and track the repair.

  1. Sign up as a chef for the quote flow.
  2. Or log in to land in a kitchen with a plan already running.
  3. Book a repair, then play the technician with the Tech simulator below.

All prices and data are made up.
